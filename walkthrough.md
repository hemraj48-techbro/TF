# Walkthrough — TiffinFinder One-Page Pitch Site

We have built a premium, modern, dark-themed one-page pitch site for **TiffinFinder** (hyperlocal tiffin subscription app for Nashik, Maharashtra), specially formatted for **Eureka! 2026 IIT Bombay E-Cell** judges and investors.

---

## 🌟 Pitch Site Highlights & Features

### 1. Hero & Eureka! 2026 E-Cell Deck Badge
- Glowing Obsidian Dark Theme (`#090A0F`) with Warm Orange accents (`#FF6B00`).
- Dedicated **Eureka! 2026 IIT Bombay E-Cell** badge with pulse animation.
- Quick Stats Ticker: 1.6L+ daily seekers, ₹50 Cr/mo TAM, 8-12% commission, Tier-2 monopoly.

### 2. The Problem Section
- **Expensive Restaurants**: ₹150–300/meal vs affordable tiffins.
- **Invisible Providers**: 500+ home cooks with zero online presence.
- **Zero Subscription Tech**: No platform offering weekly/monthly plans in Nashik.
- **Word-of-Mouth Trap**: Home cooks trapped without digital customer acquisition.

### 3. The Solution & Interactive Nashik Map Mockup
- **Hyperlocal Map Discovery**: Interactive map preview of Nashik areas (College Road, Canada Corner, Gangapur Road, Panchavati) with live provider pins.
- **7 Solution Pillars**: Map search, Weekly/Monthly plans, UPI payments, Real-time status tracking, 1-tap Pause & Skip with wallet credit, Provider dashboard, Night Tiffin Mode (9 PM–12 AM).

### 4. How It Works (3 Steps)
- Interactive step-by-step switcher:
  - **Step 1**: Discover Providers on Map
  - **Step 2**: Subscribe & Pay Online (UPI)
  - **Step 3**: Tiffin Delivered & Rated

### 5. Market Opportunity (TAM/SAM/SOM)
- **1.6 Lakh+** potential customers in Nashik (Students, PG residents, hospital staff, IT workers).
- **₹50 Crore / month TAM** in Nashik.
- **5+ Tier-2 Cities** with zero dedicated tiffin app competition (Nashik, Aurangabad, Nagpur, Kolhapur, Solapur).
- **₹7.5 Lakh Crore** India food services market size.

### 6. Business Model & Unit Economics Simulator
- **Revenue Streams**: Order commission (8–12%), Provider monthly subscription (₹199–₹499), Featured listings (₹500–₹1000/mo), Future delivery fees.
- **Interactive Financial Calculator**: Real-time sliders for active subscribers, average meal price, commission rate, and provider count to calculate monthly GMV, platform revenue, and projected ARR.

### 7. Competitive Advantage Moat Matrix
- Side-by-side comparison table highlighting **TiffinFinder vs Swiggy/Zomato vs WhatsApp local vendors**.

### 8. Future Vision & Roadmap (2025–2028)
- **2025**: Nashik Launch & 100+ Home Cooks.
- **2026**: Dominate Nashik Market (25,000 subscribers, profitability).
- **2027**: Expansion to Aurangabad & Nagpur.
- **2028**: Pan-India Tier-2 Scale & ₹500 Crore ARR target.

### 9. Eureka! 2026 Judge Feedback Hub
- Interactive modal allowing judges to rate the pitch deck, request the PDF presentation, or schedule a founder meeting.
